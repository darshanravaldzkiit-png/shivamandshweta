SUNEHRI SHAAM — WEDDING INVITATION WEBSITE
Shivam Raj Sahu & Shweta Gupta

DESIGN APPROACH
A minimal, editorial wedding invitation: real wedding/detail photography, custom-drawn burgundy-and-gold cover art, a restrained reveal-on-scroll, subtle image parallax and accessible reduced-motion behavior. No AI-generated wedding scenes are used in the page.

PACKAGE CONTENTS
- index.html: responsive one-page invitation in English and Hindi.
- assets/share-cover.png: hand-drawn editorial image for the link preview.

OPEN / PREVIEW
1. Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser. No build tools are required.
3. Photo and font URLs load from the internet, so connect to the internet when previewing.

INTERACTIONS
- Small curtain/arch intro with a skip option.
- Reveal-on-scroll and low-amplitude scroll-linked photo parallax (inspired by real editorial wedding sites, not a heavy 3D engine).
- Six ceremony cards, locality-based Google Maps links and individual .ics calendar downloads.
- Countdown to the next upcoming ceremony in India time.
- Optional synthesized ambient melody; sound starts only after the guest taps play.
- RSVP only asks the guest's name and whether they can attend. Guest count appears only for a “yes” response. The answers are formatted into WhatsApp for +91 94258 28496; the guest reviews and sends the message themselves. There is no RSVP database or background submission.
- Language toggle, share action, reduced-motion support and mobile-first layout.

BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH
1. Host index.html and assets at a public HTTPS URL. Set og:image and twitter:image to the absolute HTTPS URL of assets/share-cover.png so WhatsApp can fetch the share preview.
2. Verify the exact venue pins with the family. Current directions search the supplied localities rather than a confirmed venue coordinate.
3. Confirm any ceremony times beyond the wedding (7:00 PM onwards). Unspecified events are saved as all-day calendar entries instead of guessing a time.
4. Replace illustrative placeholder photographs with approved family / venue photos later, if available. The current photos are real photography used for atmosphere, not photos of the couple.
5. The ambient sound is synthesized audio, not a licensed recording of a shehnai or instrumental track.
